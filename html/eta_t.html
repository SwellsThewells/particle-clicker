You saw top quarks pairing up!

A very short romance

Charm quarks and their antiquarks can stick together to form the J/ψ, and beauty quarks do the same. The top quark is so heavy, and decays so quickly, that many physicists thought it would never get the chance.

In 2025 CMS saw more top quark pairs than expected among those made with only just enough energy, and ATLAS confirmed it a few months later. The excess fits toponium: a top quark and its antiquark, pulled together for the brief moment before they decay.

A quasi-bound state

A top quark lives for only about 5 × 10-25 seconds, so the pair barely has time to feel each other's pull. Physicists call it a quasi-bound state rather than a real particle.

It was long thought to be far too hard to see at the LHC. Textbooks may need a new paragraph.
