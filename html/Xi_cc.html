You discovered the Ξcc++, a baryon with two charm quarks!

Double charm

The Ξcc++ is made of two charm quarks and an up quark. With a mass of about 3621 MeV it is almost four times as heavy as the proton, which is made of two up quarks and a down quark.

LHCb announced the discovery in July 2017. It found the new particle through its decay into a Λc+ baryon, a kaon and two pions.

A double star

In most baryons the three quarks perform an elaborate dance around each other. A baryon with two heavy quarks should look more like a planetary system: the two heavy quarks orbit each other like a double star, and the light quark orbits the pair.

Measuring its properties shows how two heavy quarks and a light one hold together, a new test of quantum chromodynamics, the theory of the strong force.
