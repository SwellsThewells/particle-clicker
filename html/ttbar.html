You saw quantum entanglement between top quarks!

Spooky action at the LHC

Two particles are entangled when they share a single quantum state: measure one, and you learn something about the other, however far apart they are. Einstein called it "spooky action at a distance".

In 2023 ATLAS observed that top quarks and top antiquarks made together are entangled, and CMS confirmed it in 2024. It was the first time entanglement had been seen between quarks, and at the highest energies yet.

Reading the spins

The top quark is the heaviest particle known, and it decays before it has time to join up with other quarks. It passes its spin on to its decay products, so the angle between the electrons and muons left behind shows how the spins of the two top quarks were linked.

The entanglement is strongest when the pair is made with only just enough energy. Looking at those pairs, ATLAS found entanglement with a significance of more than five standard deviations.
