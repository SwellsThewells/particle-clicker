You studied supersymmetry!

A partner for every particle

Particles come in two families: fermions, the particles of matter such as quarks and electrons, and bosons, which carry forces, such as photons and gluons. Supersymmetry is a proposed symmetry between the two families.

If it is real, every known particle has a heavier superpartner from the other family: squarks and sleptons for the quarks and leptons, and gluinos, photinos and higgsinos for the bosons.

Why physicists like it

Supersymmetry could explain why the Higgs boson is so light, make the strengths of the forces meet at very high energies, and the lightest superpartner, the neutralino, would be a natural dark matter particle.

The LHC has been looking for superpartners since 2010. None have turned up so far, so if they exist, most of them must be heavier than the LHC can easily make. In our universe, at least.
