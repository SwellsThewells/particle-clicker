You discovered pentaquarks!

Five quarks

A pentaquark is made of four quarks and one antiquark. After several false alarms in the early 2000s, the LHCb experiment at CERN found clear signs of two pentaquarks in 2015, in decays of the Λb baryon.

They contain two up quarks, a down quark, a charm quark and an anticharm quark.

A closer look

In 2019, with far more data, LHCb found that one of them is really two particles with very similar masses, and discovered a third one. Their pattern suggests that they could be a baryon and a meson bound together, rather than five tightly packed quarks.
